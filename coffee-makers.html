How to Choose a Coffee Maker

The best coffee maker is the one that fits how you actually start your day. Start with how many cups you make and how much effort you want to spend, then match it to one of four types.

The short answer

  • Best for families and offices: A programmable drip machine with a thermal carafe
  • Best for one quick cup: A single-serve pod machine
  • Best on a budget: A French press
  • Best for cafe-style drinks: A home espresso machine

What to look for

How many cups you make

One drinker usually does best with single-serve or a French press. Two or more, or guests, suit a drip machine with a carafe.

Time and effort

Pod machines take seconds. Drip machines are close behind and can run on a timer. French press and espresso take practice but reward it.

Running costs

Pods cost more per cup than ground or whole beans. A grinder adds cost up front but improves flavor with any method.

Keeping coffee hot

A thermal carafe keeps drip coffee hot without a heating plate that slowly burns it.

Common questions

Do I need a grinder?

Not required, but freshly ground beans taste better with every method. Burr grinders give a more even grind than blade grinders.

How often should I clean it?

Rinse removable parts after each use, and descale machines with water reservoirs every few months, following the maker's instructions.
