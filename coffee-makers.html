How to Choose a Coffee Maker

Buying guide · Updated October 2026

This page contains affiliate links. If you buy through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. See our disclosure.

The best coffee maker is the one that fits how you actually start your day. Here is how the main types compare.

Drip coffee maker

Best for brewing several cups at once with minimal effort. Look for a programmable timer, a thermal carafe (keeps coffee hot without a heating plate) and an easy-to-fill reservoir.

See drip coffee makers on Amazon

Single-serve pod machine

Fastest option for one cup and almost no cleanup. The trade-off is the ongoing cost of pods and more packaging waste. Check which pod type the machine uses before buying.

See single-serve machines on Amazon

French press

Inexpensive, no electricity needed and gives a full-bodied cup. It needs coarse-ground coffee and a little more cleanup.

See French presses on Amazon

Espresso machine

Best for lattes and cappuccinos at home. Expect a learning curve and a higher upfront price. Pair it with a grinder for the best results.

See espresso machines on Amazon

How to decide

  • Busy mornings, one drinker: single-serve pod machine.
  • Family or office: programmable drip with a thermal carafe.
  • Flavor on a budget: French press.
  • Cafe-style drinks: espresso machine.
