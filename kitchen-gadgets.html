Kitchen Gadgets Worth Buying

Buying guide · Updated October 2026

This page contains affiliate links. If you buy through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. See our disclosure.

Most kitchen gadgets end up forgotten in a drawer. The ones below solve a real, repeated problem. Before buying any gadget, ask whether you will use it at least once a week and whether it replaces something you already own.

1. Instant-read digital thermometer

Takes the guesswork out of cooking meat, which improves results and food safety. Look for a fast reading, a backlit display and a probe that folds away.

See thermometers on Amazon

2. Good chef's knife

One sharp 8-inch knife does most of the work. Comfort in the hand matters more than brand name, so check reviews for balance and edge retention.

See chef's knives on Amazon

3. Silicone spatula set

Heat resistant, gentle on nonstick pans and easy to clean. Choose one-piece designs so food does not collect in the joint.

See spatula sets on Amazon

4. Airtight food storage containers

Keeps leftovers fresh and the pantry organised. Look for stackable shapes, secure lids and glass if you want to reheat in the microwave.

See storage containers on Amazon

5. Kitchen scale

Weighing ingredients gives consistent baking and easy portion control. A flat surface, a tare button and gram and ounce modes are all you need.

See kitchen scales on Amazon

Gadgets we would skip

Single-purpose tools such as egg-shaped cookers or specialty slicers usually duplicate what a knife or pan already does. Save the space and money.
