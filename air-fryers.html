How to Choose an Air Fryer

Buying guide · Updated October 2026

An air fryer is a small countertop oven that circulates hot air to crisp food with little oil. They are popular because they heat fast and are easy to use, but models differ more than the marketing suggests. These are the points that actually matter.

1. Capacity

  • 2 to 3 quarts: one person or a couple with small portions.
  • 4 to 6 quarts: the sweet spot for most households of two to four.
  • 7 quarts and up, or dual basket: families, or cooking two foods at once.

2. Basket or oven style

Basket models are compact and heat quickly. Oven-style models with racks hold more and suit baking and reheating, but they take more counter space. Dual-basket models let you cook two items with different settings and finish together.

3. Power and temperature range

Most home units run around 1,400 to 1,800 watts. A wider temperature range, ideally up to about 400°F (200°C), gives better crisping. Check that the plug and voltage in the listing match your country before you buy.

4. Controls

Dials are simple and durable. Digital screens add presets, but only helpful if you will use them. Look for a shake reminder and an auto shut-off.

5. Cleaning

A nonstick basket that is dishwasher safe saves effort over time. Read reviews for comments on coating wear, since that is the most common long-term complaint.

Frequently asked questions

Is an air fryer healthier? It uses far less oil than deep frying, so fried-style foods can have fewer calories, but the food you cook still matters most.

Can I use foil or parchment? Usually yes if it does not block airflow and is weighed down by food. Check your manual.

How do I choose between brands? Compare capacity, warranty and recent reviews on the Amazon listing, where current prices and ratings are shown.
